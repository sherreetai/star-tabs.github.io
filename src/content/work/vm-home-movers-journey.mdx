Research also showed customers needed more reassurance around availability, connectivity and what would happen during their move. <CaseSection eyebrow="Finding the friction" title="Analysed where customers were dropping out" mediaSrc="/images/vm-home-movers/friction-analysis.svg" divider="none" paddingTop="large">
  <p>I combined Contentsquare behavioural data, Tableau funnel data and 2026 user research to understand where customers were struggling and what they needed before starting their move.</p>
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection eyebrow="Designing from the research" title="Turned research into the homepage redesign" mediaSrc="/images/vm-home-movers/homepage-redesign.svg">
  <p>Research showed the homepage felt lengthy and static, so I simplified the content hierarchy around customers’ main questions and actions. I also introduced simple motion design to make key moments more engaging and break up the experience.</p>
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection eyebrow="Looking beyond the homepage" title="Redesigned the wider journey">
  <p>I used the same evidence to review the wider serviceability and booking journey. Funnel analysis showed only 15% of serviceable sessions went on to start the web booking journey, highlighting another point of friction to address.</p>
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection eyebrow="Validating the direction" title="Tested and refined key decisions" mediaSrc="/images/vm-home-movers/validation.svg" layout="split" divider="none">
  <p>I ran further research to validate parts of the redesigned experience, including how faster broadband could be communicated without distracting customers from their move. 94% of 89 participants chose one of the two prominent green notification treatments, which informed the direction for further refinement.</p>
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection eyebrow="An evidence-led direction" title="Redesigned around the 55% drop-off before postcode entry" paddingTop="large" paddingBottom="none">
  <p>Behavioural data, customer research and testing shaped the homepage and wider journey. The redesigned direction creates a clearer route through postcode entry, serviceability and booking, while keeping secondary messages focused on supporting the move.</p>
</CaseSection>
